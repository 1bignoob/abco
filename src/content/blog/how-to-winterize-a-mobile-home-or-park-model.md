<div class="winterizing-article">
<p>Properly winterizing your mobile home or park model helps protect its plumbing from freezing, burst fittings, and costly water damage. A careful seasonal closing can prevent expensive repairs and make reopening in spring much easier. Have questions? Call us at <a href="tel:+17188771197">(718) 877-1197</a>.</p>

<section class="workflow" aria-labelledby="workflow-root">
<h2 id="workflow-root">Winterize the park model</h2>
<p class="workflow-key">Follow all the steps that apply to your trailer.</p>
<ol class="workflow-steps">
<li><section aria-labelledby="shut-off">
<h3 id="shut-off">Shut off &amp; drain</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Before any draining:</strong> turn off the electric tank heater's dedicated breaker and let water cool. For tankless, follow its manual's shutdown sequence first.</li>
<li>Shut off/disconnect the main water supply; drain the supply hose. Switch off any pump per its manual.</li>
<li>Open a faucet to relieve pressure, then open approved hot/cold low-point drains. Route discharge safely away from the home.</li>
</ul>
</section></li>
<li><section aria-labelledby="water-heater">
<h3 id="water-heater">Protect the water heater</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Electric tank:</strong> with breaker off and water cool, drain and isolate/bypass exactly as its manual directs before air is connected. Do not use the relief valve as a routine drain unless instructed. Keep the breaker off until completely refilled and purged in spring.</li>
<li><strong>Gas tankless:</strong> use its exact power/gas/water shutdown and drain sequence, including designated plugs, heat exchanger and condensate components. Built-in freeze protection depends on operating conditions and does not replace seasonal draining. No air or antifreeze unless expressly permitted; use a technician for gas work.</li>
</ul>
</section></li>
<li class="air-node"><section aria-labelledby="compressed-air">
<h3 id="compressed-air">Connect regulated compressor</h3>
<p class="pressure-limit"><strong>30 PSI or less, or the lowest limit specified by any connected component, whichever is lower.</strong> This is a conservative ceiling, not universal authorization: equipment manuals and lower limits control. If air is not permitted, use the manual's alternative method.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Prerequisites:</strong> water off/disconnected, pressure relieved, low-point drains opened, heater drained and isolated/bypassed per its exact manual. Change drain positions for blowout only as the manuals direct.</li>
<li><strong>Connection:</strong> use a clean compressor suitable for potable-water plumbing at the normal main/city-water inlet, with the proper blowout adapter, regulator and gauge. Verify the regulated pressure before admitting air.</li>
<li><strong>Isolate first:</strong> dishwasher, washer, refrigerator/ice maker, filters, softeners, pumps and tankless heater, unless each exact manual expressly permits air. Their separate paths are below.</li>
<li><strong>Keep an outlet open before admitting air; never dead-head.</strong> Stop and disconnect if pressure cannot be controlled.</li>
</ul>
</section></li>
<li class="air-node"><section aria-labelledby="fixtures">
<h3 id="fixtures">Blow out each branch</h3>
<p>Use short, controlled applications on one permitted fixture/branch at a time. Open cold and hot separately; move single-handle fixtures fully cold, then fully hot. Stop airflow before switching outlets so an open escape path is always available when air resumes.</p>
<ul class="fixture-branches">
<li><strong>Kitchen faucet:</strong> cold, then hot; drain permitted pullout hose/sprayer.</li>
<li><strong>Bathroom sink:</strong> cold, then hot; drain removable parts as permitted.</li>
<li><strong>Shower:</strong> cold, then hot; cycle the diverter as its manual permits and lower/drain the hose and wand.</li>
<li><strong>Toilet supply:</strong> if its valve manual permits air, hold the flush control open until air/no water exits. Tank, bowl and trap need separate protection below.</li>
<li><strong>Exterior shower/hose bib:</strong> disconnect hoses and quick-connects; clear each supply and drain the hose/wand.</li>
<li><strong>Outdoor kitchen:</strong> clear sink hot/cold and sprayer; keep appliances isolated for their own procedures.</li>
<li id="manifold"><strong>Each labeled manifold branch:</strong> follow its hot/cold map one branch at a time, using the manifold's exact valve/drain instructions. Never force valves; record final positions. No universal ManaBloc sequence.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Disconnect air before final valve changes.</strong> No visible water does not prove every component is empty. Air does not protect traps, toilet bowls, appliance sumps or other retained-water parts.</p>
</section></li>
<li><section aria-labelledby="traps-toilet">
<h3 id="traps-toilet">Protect traps &amp; toilet</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Sink/shower traps:</strong> use compatible potable-water/RV/marine winterizing antifreeze, following label quantity and dilution directions; include other permitted drains. Preserve the liquid seal against sewer gas.</li>
<li><strong>Toilet tank, bowl and trap:</strong> remove residual water and protect separately per the toilet manual and product label. Color alone does not prove protection.</li>
<li><strong>Never automotive antifreeze.</strong> Use only equipment-compatible winterizing product; keep it away from children/pets and follow disposal instructions.</li>
</ul>
</section></li>
<li><section aria-labelledby="appliances">
<h3 id="appliances">Winterize optional appliances</h3>
<p>Keep these isolated from air unless the exact manual expressly allows it. Supply shutoff alone does not empty internal valves, pumps or sumps.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Dishwasher:</strong> shut off water/power; drain inlet, hoses, sump and pump by its manual or authorized service. Do not add antifreeze or run a cycle unless instructed.</li>
<li><strong>Washer:</strong> shut off/unplug; drain hot/cold hoses, pump/filter and drain hose as directed. Use an antifreeze amount/cycle only if that model specifies it.</li>
<li><strong>Refrigerator/ice maker:</strong> shut off water; follow its procedure for supply, inlet valve, dispenser, ice maker and filter/bypass. Check unheated-storage temperature limits.</li>
<li><strong>Filters, softeners, pumps &amp; pressure tanks:</strong> depressurize, drain/bypass and remove cartridges per each manual. Do not send air or antifreeze through without permission.</li>
<li><strong>Outdoor kitchen:</strong> separately winterize its refrigerator, ice maker, dishwasher, filter, pump or on-demand heater; inspect its low points too.</li>
</ul>
</section></li>
<li><section aria-labelledby="household-items">
<h3 id="household-items">Remove freezable items</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Food &amp; liquids:</strong> canned/bottled drinks, jars, liquid foods, medicines and toiletries.</li>
<li><strong>Stored products:</strong> paint, caulk, adhesives, cleaners, aerosols and anything labeled “protect from freezing.” Follow local hazardous-waste rules; never dump chemicals down drains.</li>
<li><strong>Temperature-sensitive equipment:</strong> batteries, power banks, tools, electronics and safety equipment outside labeled storage limits.</li>
</ul>
</section></li>
<li><section aria-labelledby="final-close">
<h3 id="final-close">Final check</h3>
<ul>
<li>Confirm water and air disconnected; check every branch, low point, appliance, trap and toilet. Wipe spills and inspect for leaks.</li>
<li>Photograph final manifold/bypass/drain positions per manuals. Label heater controls and post: <strong>“Winterized—do not energize water heater until system is full.”</strong></li>
</ul>
</section></li>
</ol>
</section>

<section aria-labelledby="spring">
<h2 id="spring">Spring reopening</h2>
<p>Inspect; restore plugs, filters and documented valve positions per manuals. Restore water slowly, purge air and check for leaks <strong>before energizing the heater</strong>; an electric tank must be completely full. Restore tankless gas/power only in its manual's order. Flush antifreeze and reopen appliances per labels/manuals; recheck after heating. Stop for leaks or abnormal gas-appliance operation and call a technician.</p>
</section>
